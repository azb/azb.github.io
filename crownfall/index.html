Crownfall

A five-by-five court. Archers on the wings, swords beside the crown. Take the enemy monarch and the court is yours.

  • Every piece steps one square, diagonally or straight, onto an empty tile.
  • Archers and swordsmen may strike an adjacent foe. The monarch only moves.
  • Win the clash and you take their square. Fall, and your piece is lost.
  • Strike the enemy monarch and the game ends.
